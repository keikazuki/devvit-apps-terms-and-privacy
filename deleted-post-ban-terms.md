# Deleted Post Ban Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-11-07

Deleted Post Ban ("the app") is a Reddit moderation helper. Once a day it checks the recent posts of a community. If
the author deleted a post after other members had already answered it, the app can give that author a temporary or
permanent ban, according to the settings chosen by the community's moderators.

1. **Who may install it.** Moderators of a community, using Reddit's Developer Platform. By installing the app you
   confirm that you are authorised to configure it for that community and that your community's rules allow this kind
   of ban.
2. **You decide the rule.** The app does nothing until configured, and it starts in **Dry run**, where it only reports
   who would be banned. Moderators choose the conditions (for example the required flair, the minimum number of
   comments and the exempt users), the ban length, the ban reason and the message. Moderators are responsible for the
   bans their community issues through the app.
3. **What the app does.** After a ban the app sends the banned user the message you configured, adds a mod note, and
   remembers the post so that it never bans for the same post twice, even if a moderator lifts the ban. It does not
   remove posts or comments. By default it never bans moderators or approved users; each exemption can be switched off.
4. **Accuracy.** The app relies on what Reddit reports about a post. A post can look deleted by its author when it is
   not (for example when an account was closed), and Reddit's reporting can change. Moderators should use Dry run
   first and review the modmail summaries. Wrongly banned users can be unbanned by moderators; the app will not ban
   them again for the same post.
5. **Posts the app can see.** The app only knows posts it saw after installation, plus posts Reddit lists to apps at
   that time. Posts deleted before installation cannot be checked.
6. **Third parties.** Reddit hosts the app and its storage and has its own terms. The app is not affiliated with or
   endorsed by Reddit.
7. **No warranty, limited liability.** The app is provided "as is". To the extent the law allows, the developer is not
   liable for losses from its use, including mistaken bans, downtime, errors or removal of the app.
8. **Changes and removal.** The developer may change or discontinue the app. Moderators can uninstall it at any time.
