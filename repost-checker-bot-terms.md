# Terms of use: Repost Checker

*Last updated: 2026-11-01. Host this page at a public address (a subreddit wiki page or GitHub Pages) and save that
address in the app's details in the Reddit developer portal.*

Repost Checker ("the app") is a free moderation tool for Reddit communities. By installing it you agree to these
terms. These are the app's own terms; they are in addition to Reddit's User Agreement, Developer Terms and Devvit
Rules.

1. **What the app does.** The app looks at posts made in the community where it is installed, compares their images,
   GIFs, video previews and links with earlier posts of the same community, and posts a comment and/or performs the
   moderation actions that the moderators chose in the app settings. Optional features (edited-copy matching, AI-image
   detection, repeat-reposter strikes, a command for users, flair, reverse-search links) do nothing until the moderators
   switch them on.
2. **Moderators are responsible.** The moderators of the community decide which actions are on (reply, remove,
   report, lock, flair, modmail, mod notes, bans) and which texts are used. Matching is automatic and can be wrong.
   Use *Restore post* to undo a mistake. The default action is a reply only. Moderators must follow Reddit's Moderator
   Code of Conduct and are responsible for rules they enforce through the app.
3. **AI detection is optional and imperfect.** If moderators turn it on, the app sends the address of a post's image
   (SightEngine) or the image itself (Google Gemini) to the service the moderators chose, using the moderators' own
   account keys, and shows or acts on the probability that comes back. Scores are estimates and can be wrong in both
   directions. The moderators are responsible for their service account, its terms, its costs and the data they send.
4. **Reverse-search links.** If moderators turn this on, the app adds links to third-party search services (Google
   Lens, TinEye, Yandex, SauceNAO, IQDB, Bing). Following a link sends the image address to that service, under its
   own terms. The app does not send anything itself.
5. **No warranty.** The app is provided "as is", without any promise that it finds every repost, that it never flags a
   different image, or that it is always available. Do not rely on it for legal or copyright decisions.
6. **Reddit rules.** You must follow the Reddit User Agreement, Content Policy, Developer Terms and the Devvit Rules.
   The app acts through its own Reddit account, which is added as a moderator of your community.
7. **Changes and removal.** The developer may change or stop the app at any time. Uninstalling the app from
   *Mod Tools → Installed apps* stops it and deletes the data it stored (see the Privacy policy).
8. **Support and reports.** Questions, problems and reports of misuse: send a message to u/kei-kazuki on Reddit.
