# Privacy policy: Flair Changer

_Last updated: 2026-11-05. Host this page at a public address and save that address in the app's details in the
Reddit developer portal._

**Who we are.** Flair Changer ("the app", registered as `sourceacquiredbot`) is a Reddit moderation app by
u/kei-kazuki. Contact: message that user on Reddit.

## What the app stores

**Nothing about users or content.** The app has no database and no storage of its own. The only things saved are the
settings the moderators of a community type into the app's settings page: up to four flair rules (flair names or IDs,
trigger words, link words, who may trigger), a starting flair, lists of link words and exact addresses, a dry-run
switch, and lists of **user names** the moderators chose (users allowed to trigger a rule, users whose comments are
approved, users who are ignored). These settings are visible to the moderators of that community, are kept until the
moderators change them or uninstall the app, and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

## What the app reads

While it handles a new comment or post, the app reads the comment's text and its author's username, the post's author
and flair, and (only for a rule limited to moderators) whether the commenter moderates the community. It uses them
only to decide whether to change a flair or approve the comment, and it does not keep them. The app's log (visible to
the developer and to moderators who run the Devvit log command) contains post and comment IDs, never usernames or
comment text.

## What leaves Reddit

Nothing. The app makes no web requests, has no external services, no analytics and no AI features.

## What the app does not do

It does not sell or share data, does not build profiles of people, does not track or surveil users, does not collect
passwords or personal information, and does not analyse faces or any biometric data.

## Deletion

A comment or post deleted by its author needs no action: the app keeps no copy. Uninstalling the app deletes the
settings of that community, including the lists of user names.

## Your choices

Moderators control every setting and can uninstall at any time. Redditors can contact the developer to ask what is
stored: the answer is the list above.
