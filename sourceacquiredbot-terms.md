# Terms of use: Flair Changer

_Last updated: 2026-11-05. Host this page at a public address (a subreddit wiki page or GitHub Pages) and save that
address in the app's details in the Reddit developer portal._

Flair Changer ("the app", registered as `sourceacquiredbot`) is a free moderation tool for Reddit communities. By
installing it you agree to these terms. They are the app's own terms, in addition to Reddit's User Agreement,
Developer Terms and Devvit Rules.

1. **What the app does.** The app reads the comments and new posts of the community where it is installed. Following
   the rules the moderators typed into the app settings, it changes a post's flair when a comment contains a trigger
   word or a link to a listed site, gives a post without flair a starting flair, and approves comments from listed
   users or with links to listed sites. It does nothing else: it does not remove, lock, report, ban or write
   comments. Until the moderators configure it, it does nothing.
2. **Moderators are responsible.** The moderators decide every rule, flair, trigger word, user list and link list, and
   whether the dry run is on. Matching is automatic and can be wrong (a link can contain a word without being what
   the moderators meant). Approving a comment overrides the removal of a filter, so list only users and sites you
   trust. Moderators can change a flair or remove a comment by hand at any time.
3. **No warranty.** The app is provided "as is", without any promise that it recognises every command or link, never
   misjudges a comment, or is always available.
4. **Reddit rules.** You must follow the Reddit User Agreement, Content Policy, Developer Terms and the Devvit Rules.
   The app acts through its own Reddit account, which is added as a moderator of your community.
5. **Changes and removal.** The developer may change or stop the app at any time. Uninstalling the app from
   _Mod Tools → Installed apps_ stops it; the app keeps no data besides its settings (see the Privacy policy).
6. **Support and reports.** Questions, problems and reports of misuse: send a message to u/kei-kazuki on Reddit.
