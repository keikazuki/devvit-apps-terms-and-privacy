# Deleted Post Ban Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07

**Summary:** Deleted Post Ban does not use analytics or advertising, does not sell or share data, and sends nothing
outside Reddit. It keeps only the few facts it needs to recognise who deleted a post and to avoid banning for the same
post twice.

## What the app handles

| Data | Why | Where it is kept | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| For each new post: its id, its author's username and the time it was posted | To know the author after the post is deleted (Reddit no longer shows it) | The app's private storage for your community | 200 days, then erased |
| For each ban: the post id, the username, the ban length, the reason and the time | To never ban twice for the same post, even if a moderator lifts the ban, and to show moderators what happened | The same storage | Until the app is uninstalled |
| The last run's summary: counts per outcome and, for each ban, the post link and username | To show moderators the result (status menu and modmail) | The same storage | Until the next run replaces it |
| Settings entered by moderators (check time, ban length, flair ID, exempt users, ban text, and so on) | App configuration | App settings | Until changed or uninstalled |

The app reads a post's flair, comment count and comments, and the moderator, approved-user and banned-user lists, only
while it checks a post. It does not keep them.

## What the app does not do

* It does not store post titles, post text or comment text.
* It does not collect browsing data, IP addresses or device information.
* It makes no requests to any website other than Reddit.
* It does not share data with other communities: each installation has its own private storage.

## Users and deleted posts

The app exists to act on posts that their authors deleted. For that reason it keeps the post id, the author's
username and the time of each post, and the record of a ban, after the author deleted the post. Moderators who do not
want this should not install the app.

## Third parties

**Reddit** hosts the app and its storage and is governed by Reddit's policies. Nothing is sent to anyone else.

## Retention

* Post records: 200 days.
* Ban records: until the app is uninstalled. Uninstalling the app removes its stored data on Reddit's side.
* Last summary: replaced at the next run.
